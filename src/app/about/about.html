The Obsidian Spire

Aethelgard was born from a singular, terrifying revelation: that memory is fragile. When the Great Fire of Oakhaven consumed the Royal Archives in 1420, it erased three centuries of lineage, arcane theory, and natural philosophy in a single night.

In the ashes, High Scholar Valerius declared that knowledge required a fortress, not merely a hall. Thus, the Obsidian Spire was raised. Built from volcanic glass and spell-forged granite, its walls are impervious to fire, flood, and the ravages of time itself.

Today, it stands not just as a repository, but as a living monument to human inquiry. Every volume added to our stacks is a defiance against forgetting.

"We are not merely guarding paper and ink; we are the wardens of civilization's soul."

— VALERIUS THE ELDER, FIRST KEEPER
